Dot Box is a polished, modern digital version of the classic pencil-and-paper game Dots and Boxes.

Challenge your strategy skills against a multi-level simulated AI, or compete locally in head-to-head Pass & Play matches with integrated win-loss scorecards.

Version 1.0.0 • Powered by HTML5, CSS3, and JS.

We respect your privacy. Dot Box operates fully local to your device:

  • No personal data or stats are shared or sent online.
  • Everything is persisted locally via Web LocalStorage.
  • Zero telemetry, trackers, or cookies are collected.
